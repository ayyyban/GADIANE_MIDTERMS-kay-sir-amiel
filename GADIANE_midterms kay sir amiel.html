About GUARDiane

GUARDiane is created because we believe everyone has the right to online privacy. Big tech companies use AI to watch everything you click, track what you do, and sell your personal information. GUARDiane keeps your data safe on your own device so you can share freely without being watched The name GUARDiane comes directly from creator Ivan Gadiane! It combines the word "GUARD" (representing security and privacy protection) with his last name "Gadiane" (GUARD + Gadiane = GUARDiane).. .
